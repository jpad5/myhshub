Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version: My HS Hub keeps everything you enter on your own device. We don't have your data, we don't collect it, and we don't share or sell it.

What the app stores

To do its job, the app stores the information you choose to enter: student profile details (such as name, school, grade, and counselor), courses, grades and assignments, test scores, activities and service hours, college lists and applications, planner items, and your appearance preferences. All of it is saved locally on your iPhone or iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. It does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking, and it does not make network connections to send your information anywhere. Because the data never leaves your device, we cannot see it, share it, or sell it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders for planner items, the app asks for permission and schedules notifications locally on your device. No notification server is involved, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Files you import and export

  • Grade imports: if you import a CSV file exported from your school's portal, it is read on your device to create courses and assignments. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Backups: if you save a backup, the app creates a file and hands it to the system share or save sheet. It goes only where you choose, such as Files, AirDrop, or email. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Sharing: if you share your service-hour log, the text is passed to the share sheet and sent only to the destination you pick.

Backups made by Apple

Your device may be backed up by Apple's iCloud or by your computer according to your own iOS settings. If so, the app's data can be included in those backups. That is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Children and students

My HS Hub is designed for high school students and the families who support them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and because all information stays on the device, no personal information is transmitted to us.

Deleting your data

You can delete a student profile inside the app, which removes its records. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact
