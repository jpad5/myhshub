Support

Help with My HS Hub

Need a hand? Email admin@myenterpriseai.com and describe what happened, including your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version. Please don't send grades or other personal details unless we ask.

Take the tour

In the app, open Settings (the gear icon), then Help, to replay the App Tour or read the How to Use guide.

Import your grades

  1. In your school's grade portal, export your assignments and grades as a CSV file. Most portals, including Infinite Campus, offer an export or a printable list you can save as CSV.
  2. In My HS Hub, go to Academics, then Import Gradebook, and choose your file.
  3. Review the number of courses and assignments found, then tap Import.

Importing the same file later updates scores only. Categories and multipliers you set by hand are kept. Rows that are resources, dropped assignments, or not yet scored are skipped automatically.

Set up grade weights

Weights are optional. When you add or edit a course, use Grade Weights to add categories such as Tests or Homework with a percentage each. A category's multiplier scales how much it counts, so weight 20 with a 2x multiplier counts as 40%. Without categories, the course uses a simple average.

Back up and restore

Open Settings and choose Save Backup to Device. To restore, choose Restore from Backup File and pick the file, then decide whether to add to existing data or replace it. Moving to a new device? Move the file with AirDrop or Files and restore it there.

Free trial and unlock

My HS Hub is free to try for 7 days from the day you first install it. After that it is view-only until you make the one-time $9.99 purchase: you can still see all your data and save a backup, but adding, editing, and importing are paused. Reinstalling the app does not restart the trial. To bring the purchase to a new device, open Settings and tap Restore Purchase, using the same Apple Account.

Where the college numbers come from

Acceptance rates and SAT and ACT ranges come from the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard (release of Nov. 2, 2026). The app does not use U.S. News rankings. SAT ranges add each section's percentiles, so they are approximate, and test-blind schools such as the University of California campuses report no scores. Numbers change every year, so confirm with each college. You can enter your own rank for any college.

About the GPA numbers

The UC GPA follows the University of California's published method. The Non-UC GPA uses the same grades with broader honors eligibility, and the High School GPA covers every graded course on a standard scale. All three are estimates for planning. Always confirm with your counselor and the colleges you apply to. You can hide the extra GPA cards in Settings.

Reminders aren't showing up

Check that notifications are allowed for My HS Hub in iOS Settings, and that the planner item has a reminder turned on and a due date in the future.
